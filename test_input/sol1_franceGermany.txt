During World War II, Germany and France were **not** allies. In fact, they were on opposing sides. Germany, along with Italy and Japan, formed the Axis powers. France, along with Great Britain, the United States, the Soviet Union, and China, were part of the Allies¹². France declared war on Germany two days after the German invasion of Poland in 1939. However, France was later occupied by Germany during the war. The "Free French Forces" continued to fight alongside the Allies, but the official French government was under German control until liberation.
